Reports

Active and Upcoming Notice Reports

Active Workflow Report

Provides user with a list of all their notices and what step of the workflow they are in.

Renewal Report

Gives a list of contracts that are going to end in the specified time frame for the specified institution.

Past Due Report

List of procurements that have been on the needs reminder list for over 60 days.

Bulletin Activity Reports

Shared Offers

List of procurements that have been designated during the award process that the XXXX of the contract can be utilized by other institutions.

Award by Institution

List of vendors that were awarded during a specific time frame.

Vendor Awards by Time

Shows the total number of awards and total award amounts for a specific date range and university selected.

Number of Transactions by Action Type

The total number of actions occurred for a given institution for a specified date range.

Justification Report

Lists the selected justifications for either Emergency, Exemption or Sole Source Notice types.

SPO Responses Report

Provides a list of procurements for the selected time frame and the date and times that they were sent to the SPO and when the SPO responded to them.

BEP/Veteran/Small Business Report

Provides a list of procurements for the selected time frame and institution, and gives the awarded vendor information, and whether or not they are a BEP/Veteran/Small Business and the percentage and dollar amount.

Vendor Document and Email Reports

Vendor Document Request History

Provides what procurements a specific vendor downloaded documents for.

Notice Document Request History

Provides a list of vendors that downloaded documents for a specific procurement notice.

Email Notifications Sent to Vendor for Notice Report

List of vendors that were sent email notifications for a specific procurement number.

Vendors Signed up for Email Notification Report

List of vendors that are signed up to receive emails based on the NIGP codes.
