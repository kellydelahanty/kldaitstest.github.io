Border Style Examples

  • Row borders
    • By default, rows will have darker gray lines between them
    • row-border - Datatables default, light gray row borders
  • Column borders
    • v-grid - black vertical column borders
    • v-grid-gray - gray vertical column borders
  • Cell borders
    • cell-border - Datatables default, light gray cell borders
    • cell-border-dark - Black cell borders
  • Table borders
    • tableBorder - black border around entire talbe
    • tableBorder_gray - gray border around entire talbe
  • tableBorder
