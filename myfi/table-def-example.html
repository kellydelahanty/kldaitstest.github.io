C-FOAPAL
The Chart of Accounts structure is composed of the following segments: Chart, Fund, Organization, Account, Program, Activity (Optional), Location (Optional). A valid C-FOAPAL is required to post or query transactions and to retrieve financial reports.
Chart
Identifies the chart within the C-FOAPAL: 1=Urbana-Champaign, 2=Chicago, 4=Springfield, 9=System Offices
Fund
Identifies the fund used within the C-FOAPAL, which denotes the funding source of the financial resources. Common examples include state funds (1000YY), Indirect Cost Recovery (ICR) funds (200250), self-supporting funds (3nnnnn), grant funds (4nnnnn & 5nnnn), gift funds (6nnnnn), plant funds (7nnnnn), loan funds (8nnnnn), and custodial funds (9nnnnn).
Organization
Identifies the organization used within the C-FOAPAL, which denotes the department that is fiscally responsible for the financial resources.
Program
Identifies the program code used within the C-FOAPAL, which denotes the functional classification of the financial activity (such as instruction or research) as required by the National Association of College and University Business Officers (NACUBO).
Grant
A unique identifier assigned in Banner to each sponsored project. Note: The fund number associated with this activity may represent only a portion of the grant code's financial scope. Additional fund numbers could also be linked to the grant code. Please review the full grant details for comprehensive information.
Title
Identifies the title of the program code if the C-FOAPAL uses a shared fund code (such as a state or ICR – Indirect Cost Recovery fund). Otherwise, it identifies the title of the fund if the C-FOAPAL uses a unique fund code (such as a self-supporting, grant, gift, or custodial fund).
Ind
Identifies the Banner indicator, which denotes whether the financial activity is year-to-date (YTD) or inception-to-date (ITD, which aggregates the total financial activity since the inception of the C-FOAPAL).
Budget
Identifies the budgeted amount for either YTD (year-to-date) or ITD (inception-to-date) financial activity, depending on the time period of the Ind column.
Current Month Expenses
Amount of expenses recorded during the selected period (month) and fiscal year.
Expenses
Amount of expenses recorded either year-to-date (YTD) or inception-to-date (ITD), depending on the option selected within the Indicator (Ind) column.
Encumbrances
Identifies the obligated commitment balance for upcoming expenditures from requisitions, purchase orders, payroll encumbrances, and general encumbrances.
Budget Balance Available
Identifies the budget balance available to spend. This is calculated by taking the net available budget and deducting the year-to-date expenses and encumbrances.
